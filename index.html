04 / UNDER THE CANOPY

Small by design.
Built with care.

No download launcher. No account. Just a WebGL world running in your browser, with a few considered choices behind the scenery.

TYPESCRIPT / THREE.JS / VITE
A world, one chunk at a time+

The landscape is organized into 16×16 chunks, loaded around you as you move. Only exposed block faces are meshed, so the browser isn't drawing a separate cube for every piece of earth.

NEARBY TERRAIN IN. DISTANT TERRAIN OUT.
A familiar seed, a varied landscape+

Seed 8317 shapes Stillwater Basin's beaches, trees, hills, mountains, water and cave-like pockets. Seeded terrain generation keeps it repeatable; procedural block clouds finish the sky.

SAME SEED. YOUR OWN WAY THROUGH.
Blocks that really change the world+

Breaking and placing update stored block data and rebuild the affected chunk meshes, including shared borders. Edits survive walking away and back during your session. Reloading the tab starts fresh.

REAL EDITS, NOT A VISUAL TRICK.
Room to turn the detail down+

View distance ranges from 2 to 24 chunks, with a default of 12 (192 blocks). Start lower on a smaller laptop. Shared materials, disposable geometry and instanced clouds keep the prototype modest; generating chunks can still cause a hitch.

FIELD BUILD 0.1 / PLAYABLE PROTOTYPE

A beginning.
Not a finished game.

Wildmere is a compact creative-mode slice. Enough to explore and make something, with its rough edges out in the open.

Here today

Terrain to explore, blocks to edit, creative flight, collision, settings, and photographs of the places you find.

Worth knowing

Your builds last for this tab's session. No saved worlds, crafting, multiplayer or touch controls yet. Water is a static surface, not a fluid simulation.

The next useful steps

Persistent saves, smoother chunk generation, and more variety in the trees and terrain. Directions to explore, not a release promise.
