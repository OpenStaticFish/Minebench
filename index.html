04 BUILD NOTES

Under the surface.

A /

One seed, many chunks

Coordinates and seed 1847 determine the river, ridges, and tree cover. The game streams 16 × 16-block chunks around you; the view-distance slider sets the radius from three to nine chunks.

B /

Skip the buried faces

The Three.js renderer draws one mesh per chunk, leaving buried block faces out. An edit rebuilds the affected geometry, including faces revealed across a chunk border.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Playable alpha 0.1.0

A local, single-player creative prototype. Your blocks stay put when you leave a chunk, but refreshing the tab resets the world. Settings survive a reload.
