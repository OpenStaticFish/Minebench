04 / A PEEK UNDER THE TERRAIN

Small blocks.
Thoughtful foundations.

A lightweight creative slice built with TypeScript and Three.js. The world is procedural; its building blocks are kept deliberately simple.

16×16

A world in manageable pieces

Terrain loads in chunks around you. Only exposed block faces are drawn, rather than rendering a separate cube for every block.

74821

A repeatable starting point

Seeded generation brings you back to the same coast. Your edits live in voxel data and survive chunk unloading during the session.

2—5

Choose your horizon

Adjust the chunk view radius for your hardware. Tune mouse sensitivity, switch to flight, and capture the canvas with one key.

05 / STILL GROWING

A prototype.
With room to grow.

This is an early creative-mode experiment, not a finished survival game. The playable core is here. The rest is an open horizon.

ALPHA 0.1Playable now

Here today

Generated terrain, editable blocks, collision and jumping, creative flight, a hotbar, pause menu, settings, and world screenshots.

Know before you wander

Edits last for this tab’s session; refreshing resets the world. Water is decorative. There’s no crafting, combat, multiplayer, or touch control.

The next useful steps

Persistent saves, smoother chunk meshing, richer terrain shading, swimming, and sound are sensible next improvements—not promised release dates.
