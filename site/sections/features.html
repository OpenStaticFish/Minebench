Features

What is actually in the build

No feature list on a slide — every item below has a working code path you can reach from the main menu.

Procedural voxel terrain

Layered noise shapes continents, rolling lowlands and ridged mountains up to the 92-block build ceiling. Every column is generated from the seed, not hand-placed.

Chunk-based streaming

The world is a grid of 16×16 columns that generate and mesh around you on a per-frame time budget, then unload when you leave. View distance is adjustable from 2 to 10 chunks.

Block breaking & placing

Raycast editing with instant feedback, break particles and a 9-slot hotbar. Edits are written into world data and survive chunk unloads, so what you build stays built.

Creative flight

Double-tap Space or press F to fly, with a HUD badge showing the state. Sprint through the sky, land anywhere, and toggle back to gravity with the same key.

Walking, jumping & swimming

Gravity, jumping and sprinting resolved as AABB collisions against the voxel grid — no physics engine, just predictable movement. Water slows you down and lets you swim up.

Biome variation

Temperature and moisture noise drive plains, forests, deserts and snow-capped peaks. Beaches appear where land meets water, and deserts grow cacti instead of trees.

Caves & carved terrain

3D noise carves tunnels and pockets below the surface. Hillsides open into cave mouths you can walk or fly into, and digging down can break straight into a cavern.

Water, beaches & shorelines

Translucent, swimmable water at sea level with a subtle animated surface, underwater fog, and sand that follows the shoreline. Water surfaces stay continuous across chunk borders.

Sky, clouds & fog

A gradient sky dome with a sun glow, drifting blocky clouds that can be toggled off, and distance fog tied to your view setting so chunk pop-in is hidden behind the haze.

Live settings & capture

Change view distance, mouse sensitivity, field of view and render scale while playing, toggle clouds, and press P to save a PNG of the current frame.
