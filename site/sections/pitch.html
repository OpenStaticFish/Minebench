About the game

A small world that behaves like a world

Cubewild builds its terrain from a numeric seed. Layered noise shapes rolling plains, forests, deserts and snow-capped peaks; 3D noise carves caves underneath; the sea fills everything below Y = 62. The same seed always rebuilds the same coastline.

The prototype is built around the feel of a creative sandbox: responsive movement, instant block editing, creative flight, and a world that streams in around you as you explore. Dig a tunnel through a hill, fly over the water, then rebuild the shoreline block by block.

Everything on screen is generated at runtime — terrain, textures, sky and clouds. There are no external art packs and no copyrighted assets from other games.

Cubewild is an early prototype, not a finished game. The status section is honest about what works today and what does not.
