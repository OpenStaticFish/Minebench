Technical highlights

Built like a small engine

Under the HUD this is a real voxel renderer rather than a scene of loose cubes. Here is what is happening each frame.

  • Face-culled chunk meshing. Only block faces that touch air or water are emitted, with per-vertex ambient occlusion and directional shading baked into vertex colours. No shadow maps, no per-cube draw calls.
  • Deterministic generation. Layered Perlin noise plus integer hashing makes every chunk a pure function of seed and coordinates, so chunks generate identically in any order and a seed always rebuilds the same world.
  • Streaming with a budget. Chunks generate and mesh from a distance-sorted queue on a per-frame time slice. Far geometry is disposed, and player edits are stored separately and replayed when a chunk regenerates.
  • Water as a second pass. Translucent water is its own mesh per chunk with a world-space vertex wave, so the surface stays continuous across chunk borders.
  • Verified behaviour. 29 unit tests cover noise, worldgen, meshing and world edits, and a Playwright smoke test drives real mouse and keyboard input to verify breaking, placing, flight, pausing and live settings changes.
  • ~2.2 msto generate one chunk
  • 2 meshesper chunk, opaque + water

Built with Bun, Vite, strict TypeScript and Three.js. Textures, sky and clouds are generated procedurally in code — the project ships no external art.
